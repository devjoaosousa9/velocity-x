06 — The idea

Built forthe spacebetweenstrides.

Most shoes are designed for the moment your foot hits the ground. Velocity X was designed for the moment it leaves. Every gram, every millimetre of foam, every lug exists to shorten the time between one stride and the next.

The result is a shoe that behaves less like equipment and more like an extension of motion: a rockered geometry that rolls you forward, a heel that absorbs and returns, an upper that disappears.

“We stopped asking how to make it faster. We asked what was slowing it down — and removed it.”

— Velocity Lab, design notes (fictional)
