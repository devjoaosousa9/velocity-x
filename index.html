Up close

1 of 4

One knit, two overlays

The upper is a single engineered knit with two fused overlays across the midfoot. Heel to toe it measures 298 mm, straight off the scan.

2 of 4

Pull loops, front and back

A fabric loop at the heel and another on the tongue get the shoe on without crushing the collar. The collar is padded and lined in black.

3 of 4

Laces that wrap the midfoot

Round laces run through four reinforced eyelets a side, held centred by the tongue loop. The widest point is 116 mm across the forefoot.

4 of 4

Foam from heel to toe

A one-piece foam midsole with a rounded toe. From the ground to the top of the heel loop the shoe stands 152 mm tall.
